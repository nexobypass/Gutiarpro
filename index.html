Your Path to Learn Guitar

Five clear stages — from holding the guitar for the first time to playing full songs. Check off each milestone at your own pace.

1

Foundations

Weeks 1–2 • Getting comfortable
  • How to hold the guitar (seated & standing)
  • Names of the 6 strings: E A D G B e
  • Right hand: holding a pick, basic downstrokes
  • Left hand: finger numbering (1-2-3-4)
  • Tuning your guitar with this tuner
  • Finger stretch exercise 1-2-3-4 per string
Progress0%
2

First Chords

Weeks 3–6 • Open position
  • Em, Am, D — the easy 2-3 finger chords
  • G, C, E, A — full open chords
  • The "anchor finger" concept (G–C–G transition)
  • Switching between 2 chords in 5 seconds
  • Simple 4/4 downstroke strum pattern
  • Play your first 2-chord song (Em → Am)
Progress0%
3

Rhythm & Strumming

Weeks 7–12 • Groove & timing
  • Down-up strumming (D D U U D U)
  • The "Wonderwall" strum pattern
  • Using a metronome at 60-90 BPM
  • Muting strings for percussive hits
  • 3/4 vs 4/4 time signatures
  • Playing along to a full song start-to-finish
Progress0%
4

Barre Chords & Scales

Months 4–6 • Unlocking the fretboard
  • F barre chord (the big milestone!)
  • E-shape and A-shape movable barre chords
  • Pentatonic minor scale (box 1)
  • Pentatonic major scale (box 1)
  • Basic hammer-ons and pull-offs
  • Your first 12-bar blues in A
Progress0%
5

Songs & Style

Month 6+ • Finding your voice
  • 5 full songs in your favorite genre
  • Fingerpicking patterns (Travis picking)
  • Capo usage and transposing keys
  • Bending, slides, and vibrato
  • Improvising over a backing track
  • Record yourself and share your first cover

Pro Tips for Beginners

Shortcuts that save months of frustration.

⏱️

15 minutes daily beats 2 hours weekly

Muscle memory builds through frequency, not marathon sessions. Short, focused practice every day wins.

👂

Always tune before practicing

Playing out of tune trains your ear wrong. Open this tuner first — every single session.

🐌

Slow down to speed up

Practice transitions at half speed. Speed comes automatically once the movement is clean.

💪

F barre chord is a wall — push through

Everyone struggles with barre chords for 2–4 weeks. It's normal. Don't quit at stage 4.

🎵

Learn songs, not just exercises

Songs keep you motivated. Pick one easy song per week and learn it completely.

📸

Record yourself weekly

You won't notice daily progress, but recordings a month apart will shock you.
